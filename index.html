Machine learning researcher, UNC Charlotte

I build methods that move probability mass where it should go: optimal transport for comparing and aligning datasets, and finetuned diffusion models that steer protein generation toward better antibodies.

Research

Generative models for antibody design

I work on finetuning diffusion models for antibody CDR design using Reinforcment Learning. More Specifically, use policy gradient methods to finetune discrete time diffusion models.

Optimal transport for learning

My PhD work at the University of Delaware used partial, neural and sliced optimal transport to select subsets, align distributions, and find where two datasets disagree under covariate shift.

Before machine learning, I worked on Lyapunov and optimal control of quantum gates and open quantum systems.
